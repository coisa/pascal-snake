## Ownership

The repository owner decides scope. Plans describe implementation; they do
not independently authorize external actions.

## Local Contracts

Write in English. Keep one current recipe for this modernization, with
artifacts, steps, acceptance evidence, constraints and rollback. Link real
issues and avoid invented approvals or results. Git retains revisions.

## Work Guidance

Update the existing plan before material changes. Map requirements to
validation and keep the final recipe consistent with the implementation.

## Verification

Check paths, dependencies, acceptance criteria and external-action boundaries.
